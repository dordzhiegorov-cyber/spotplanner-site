Our packs

The destination packs the app downloads — the selection of spots and the derived defaults — are published at github.com/dordzhiegorov-cyber/spotplanner-packs under CC BY-SA 4.0, with the credits above. The ranking shown in the app (the Spot Score) is computed on your device from these open signals.

What is not used

No Google Places data is stored or ranked; Google content appears only inside Google's own cards. No Instagram data is collected. No content is scraped.

Corrections and takedowns

A wrong fact is best fixed at the source (Wikidata, OpenStreetMap, Wikivoyage), where it helps every app. A photo you own and want removed: hello@spotplanner.eu, handled the same day.
