Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026. Applies to the Spotplanner app for iPhone, Android and the web at spotplanner.eu.

The short version

Spotplanner does not have accounts and does not collect personal data. Everything you create in the app — your trips, your notes, the photos you add, the countries you pin, your name and home country — is stored on your device only. We do not see it, and we do not sell or share anything, because we have nothing to share.

What stays on your device

  • Your trips, their dates, notes and the photos you attach to them.
  • Your travel pins: countries, dates, places, notes and photos in your diary.
  • Your profile: the name you typed, your home country, your language and your map preferences.
  • Downloaded destination packs (the list of spots for a city), so the app works offline.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. On the web, clearing the browser's site data does the same.

What the app requests from the internet

To show content, the app downloads public files. These requests carry your IP address and the name of the file, like any web page you open; no account, name or trip data is attached.

Instagram top posts

Once a week, Spotplanner's own Instagram account asks Instagram's official API for the top public posts of a fixed list of place hashtags (for example #vondelpark) — never for people or brands. We keep two things: aggregate numbers per spot (how many top posts, their median likes and comments), and the links (permalinks) of a spot's three most-engaged top posts for at most 30 days, so the app can show them through Instagram's own embed. We do not keep captions, images, user names or anything about who posted. To ask for a post to be removed sooner, write to hello@spotplanner.eu.

Location

If you tap "Share my location" on the suggestion screen, the app reads your position once to show how far each spot is. It is used on the device only and never sent anywhere. You can decline, and the app works without it.

Photos

Photos you add are copied into the app's own folder on your device. They are used only inside the app, in the pictures and videos you choose to share yourself.

Sharing

Pictures, videos and text you share go through your device's share sheet to the app you pick (Instagram, Messages, …). Spotplanner does not post anything on your behalf and does not receive what you share.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking identifiers, no third-party SDKs that profile you.
  • No Google Places data is stored by the app; Google content appears only inside Google's own cards.
  • No Instagram data about you is collected; the app never reads your Instagram account.

Children

The app is rated for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.

When accounts arrive

A future version will offer an optional account to sync trips between devices. That version will update this policy before anything leaves your device, and the app will keep working without an account.
