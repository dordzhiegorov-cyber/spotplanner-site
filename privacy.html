Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026. Applies to the Spotplanner app for iPhone, Android and the web at spotplanner.eu.

The short version

Spotplanner works without an account: everything you create — your trips, your notes, the photos you add, the countries you pin, your name and home country — is stored on your device. If you choose to create an account, your trips, your world map and your profile are also kept on our server so they sync between your devices and survive a new phone, together with the one photo of your own you put on each stop of a trip (other photos never leave the device). We do not sell or share anything, we run no analytics and no advertising, and you can delete your account inside the app at any time.

If you create an account (optional)

You can sign in with an email address and a password. Then we store, on our server:

  • Your email address, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), the name you typed, and the date the account was created.
  • Your trips (destination, dates, chosen spots, your own places and notes), your travel pins (countries, dates, places, notes) and your profile (home country, language, map preferences).
  • On iPhone and Android, the photo of your own you add to a stop of a trip ("Add my photo"; one per stop for now), as a reduced copy (JPEG, at most 2048 pixels on the long side). Other photos — a trip's own photos, diary photos — are not uploaded. See "Photos" below.
  • Sign-in sessions (which device, when) so you stay signed in, and short-lived password-reset tokens when you ask for one.

Where: on Google Cloud in the Netherlands (europe-west4), encrypted in transit and at rest, with daily backups kept for 7 days. Who sees it: only the app on your devices, through your sign-in; we read it only to run the service or if you ask for help. Emails (a sign-up code, a password-reset link) are sent through Resend (EU region, Ireland), which acts as our processor and sees only your address and that message.

Deleting: "Delete account" in the account sheet removes your account, your sessions and everything synced, immediately and for good; your stored photos are deleted from the bucket right after (at the latest within a few hours, if storage is briefly unavailable); backups expire within 7 days. The copy on your device stays until you delete the app. You can also write to hello@spotplanner.eu. Legal basis under the GDPR: performance of the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)); controller: Dordzhi Egorov, Netherlands.

Without an account nothing of the above is collected, and the app keeps working the same way. The "Back up to a file" button creates a file on your device that you keep wherever you like; we never receive it.

What stays on your device

  • Your trips, their dates, notes and the photos you attach to them (with an account, the photo of a stop is kept there too; see "Photos").
  • Your travel pins: countries, dates, places, notes and photos in your diary.
  • Your profile: the name you typed, your home country, your language and your map preferences.
  • Downloaded destination packs (the list of spots for a city), so the app works offline.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. On the web, clearing the browser's site data does the same.

What the app requests from the internet

To show content, the app downloads public files. These requests carry your IP address and the name of the file, like any web page you open; no account, name or trip data is attached.

Instagram top posts

Once a week, Spotplanner's own Instagram account asks Instagram's official API for the top public posts of a fixed list of place hashtags (for example #vondelpark) — never for people or brands. We keep two things: aggregate numbers per spot (how many top posts, their median likes and comments), and the links (permalinks) of a spot's three most-engaged top posts for at most 30 days, so the app can show them through Instagram's own embed. We do not keep captions, images, user names or anything about who posted. To ask for a post to be removed sooner, write to hello@spotplanner.eu.

Location

If you tap "Share my location" on the suggestion screen, the app reads your position once to show how far each spot is. It is used on the device only and never sent anywhere. You can decline, and the app works without it.

Photos

Photos you add are copied into the app's own folder on your device. They are used only inside the app, in the pictures and videos you choose to share yourself. Without an account they are not uploaded anywhere.

With an account (signed in, email address confirmed), on iPhone and Android, the one photo of your own you put on a stop of a trip is also kept in your account, so your other devices show it too:

  • What: a reduced copy of that photo (JPEG, at most 2048 pixels on the long side), linked to your account, the trip and the spot. One photo per stop for now; a new one replaces the old one, which is deleted. Trip photos and diary photos are not uploaded.
  • Where: in a private storage bucket on Google Cloud in the Netherlands (europe-west4), encrypted in transit and at rest. It is never public.
  • Who can see it: only you, through the app on your signed-in devices; each download uses a link that works for a few minutes only. We do not look at your photos, use them for anything else, or share them.
  • How long: until you remove the photo or the trip (the copy is then deleted; a photo no trip of yours names any more is deleted after 14 days at the latest), or until you delete your account.

The photos you pick for a day of your trip blog are kept in your account the same way (a reduced JPEG copy, at most 10 per trip for now, linked to your account and the trip), so your devices and a follow link you switch on can show them. They are deleted when you remove them, when nothing of yours names them any more (after 14 days at the latest), or with your account.

On the web, photos you add stay in that browser tab only and are not uploaded.

Feedback

If you send feedback with the Feedback tab, we store your message, the kind you picked (idea, problem, other) and, to understand the note, the screen you were on, the app version, your device platform and the app language; if you are signed in, your account. It is stored on our server (Google Cloud, Netherlands) and emailed to the Spotplanner team through Resend. We keep it until it has been dealt with and then for at most 12 months; ask us to delete it sooner at hello@spotplanner.eu.

Places you share

When you add a place of your own to a trip, its name, its location on the map, how long you plan to stay and the destination are sent to our server (Google Cloud, Netherlands), with your account if you are signed in, so the place can be added to that destination for other travellers. Your photos are never sent. Nobody else sees the place until the Spotplanner team has reviewed and approved it; approved places become part of the public destination guide (without any link to you). Places that are not approved are deleted within 12 months. A place you only call "My place" is not sent. Ask us to remove a place at hello@spotplanner.eu.

Sharing

Pictures, videos and text you share go through your device's share sheet to the app you pick (Instagram, Messages, …). Spotplanner does not post anything on your behalf and does not receive what you share.

Follow link: if you switch on "Follow link" for a trip (this needs an account), a copy of that trip — its title, destination and dates, each day's route and stops with their times, the words you wrote in its blog, the blog photos you picked, and your first name if you chose to show it — is kept on our server and can be seen by anyone who has the link, at spotplanner.eu/trip.html, without an account. The app updates the copy while you edit the trip. Anyone you give the link to can pass it on, so share it with care. It stays visible until you switch the link off (the copy and the link are then deleted at once) or make a new link (the old one stops working), or until you delete your account. The page sets no cookies and does no tracking.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking identifiers, no third-party SDKs that profile you.
  • No data is used for anything but running the app: no marketing, no profiling, no selling, no sharing beyond the processors named above.
  • No Google Places data is stored by the app; Google content appears only inside Google's own cards.
  • No Instagram data about you is collected; the app never reads your Instagram account.

Children

The app is rated for everyone. Accounts are optional and meant for adults; we do not knowingly keep an account for a child under 16, and delete one on request.
