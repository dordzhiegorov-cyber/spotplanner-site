Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026. Applies to the Spotplanner app for iPhone, Android and the web at spotplanner.eu.

The short version

Spotplanner works without an account: everything you create — your trips, your notes, the photos you add, the countries you pin, your name and home country — is stored on your device. If you choose to create an account, your trips, your world map and your profile are also kept on our server so they sync between your devices and survive a new phone; photos never leave the device. We do not sell or share anything, we run no analytics and no advertising, and you can delete your account inside the app at any time.

If you create an account (optional)

You can sign in with an email address and a password. Then we store, on our server:

  • Your email address, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), the name you typed, and the date the account was created.
  • Your trips (destination, dates, chosen spots, your own places and notes), your travel pins (countries, dates, places, notes) and your profile (home country, language, map preferences). Photos are not uploaded.
  • Sign-in sessions (which device, when) so you stay signed in, and short-lived password-reset tokens when you ask for one.

Where: on Google Cloud in the Netherlands (europe-west4), encrypted in transit and at rest, with daily backups kept for 7 days. Who sees it: only the app on your devices, through your sign-in; we read it only to run the service or if you ask for help. Emails (a sign-up code, a password-reset link) are sent through Resend (EU region, Ireland), which acts as our processor and sees only your address and that message.

Deleting: "Delete account" in the account sheet removes your account, your sessions and everything synced, immediately and for good; backups expire within 7 days. The copy on your device stays until you delete the app. You can also write to hello@spotplanner.eu. Legal basis under the GDPR: performance of the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)); controller: Dordzhi Egorov, Netherlands.

Without an account nothing of the above is collected, and the app keeps working the same way. The "Back up to a file" button creates a file on your device that you keep wherever you like; we never receive it.

What stays on your device

  • Your trips, their dates, notes and the photos you attach to them.
  • Your travel pins: countries, dates, places, notes and photos in your diary.
  • Your profile: the name you typed, your home country, your language and your map preferences.
  • Downloaded destination packs (the list of spots for a city), so the app works offline.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. On the web, clearing the browser's site data does the same.

What the app requests from the internet

To show content, the app downloads public files. These requests carry your IP address and the name of the file, like any web page you open; no account, name or trip data is attached.

Instagram top posts

Once a week, Spotplanner's own Instagram account asks Instagram's official API for the top public posts of a fixed list of place hashtags (for example #vondelpark) — never for people or brands. We keep two things: aggregate numbers per spot (how many top posts, their median likes and comments), and the links (permalinks) of a spot's three most-engaged top posts for at most 30 days, so the app can show them through Instagram's own embed. We do not keep captions, images, user names or anything about who posted. To ask for a post to be removed sooner, write to hello@spotplanner.eu.

Location

If you tap "Share my location" on the suggestion screen, the app reads your position once to show how far each spot is. It is used on the device only and never sent anywhere. You can decline, and the app works without it.

Photos

Photos you add are copied into the app's own folder on your device. They are used only inside the app, in the pictures and videos you choose to share yourself. They are not part of the account sync and are not uploaded anywhere.

Feedback

If you send feedback with the Feedback tab, we store your message, the kind you picked (idea, problem, other), the email you leave for a reply if you leave one, and, to understand the note, the screen you were on, the app version, your device platform and the app language; if you are signed in, your account. It is stored on our server (Google Cloud, Netherlands) and emailed to the Spotplanner team through Resend. We keep it until it has been dealt with and then for at most 12 months; ask us to delete it sooner at hello@spotplanner.eu.

Places you share

When you add a place of your own to a trip, its name, its location on the map, how long you plan to stay and the destination are sent to our server (Google Cloud, Netherlands), with your account if you are signed in, so the place can be added to that destination for other travellers. Your photos are never sent. Nobody else sees the place until the Spotplanner team has reviewed and approved it; approved places become part of the public destination guide (without any link to you). Places that are not approved are deleted within 12 months. A place you only call "My place" is not sent. Ask us to remove a place at hello@spotplanner.eu.

Sharing

Pictures, videos and text you share go through your device's share sheet to the app you pick (Instagram, Messages, …). Spotplanner does not post anything on your behalf and does not receive what you share.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking identifiers, no third-party SDKs that profile you.
  • No data is used for anything but running the app: no marketing, no profiling, no selling, no sharing beyond the processors named above.
  • No Google Places data is stored by the app; Google content appears only inside Google's own cards.
  • No Instagram data about you is collected; the app never reads your Instagram account.

Children

The app is rated for everyone. Accounts are optional and meant for adults; we do not knowingly keep an account for a child under 16, and delete one on request.
