Support

Write to hello@spotplanner.eu. You will get an answer from the person who makes the app, usually within a day or two.

Common questions

A destination says it "needs a connection the first time".

Each destination's spots are a small download (usually under 100 KB) the first time you open it. After that it works offline. Connect once, open the destination again, and it stays on your phone.

My city is not in the list.

Nearly 1,300 destinations are included and more are added every month. Tell us which one you miss and we add it to the next run.

Opening hours or a price look wrong.

Hours come from OpenStreetMap and Wikivoyage; where no source had them, the app uses the usual hours for that kind of place. Always check the official site before you go — the link is on the spot's page. Corrections are welcome by email, and better still on OpenStreetMap or Wikivoyage, where they help everyone.

Why is this spot ranked so high?

Tap "Why it's famous" on the spot page: every number names its source — photos on Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia readers per month, how many languages have an article. There are no paid placements.

Where are my trips stored?

On your device. Everything works without an account. If you want your trips on more than one device, create a free account in the account sheet (the round button at the top right), with an email address and a password, with Google, or with Apple on iPhone. Once your address is confirmed, your trips, pins and profile sync between your devices and spotplanner.eu, and the one photo of your own on each stop is kept with them. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: account sheet → Delete account. Your account and everything synced to it are removed from our server for good. The copy on your device stays, so you can keep using Spotplanner without an account. You can also write to us.

The photo of a place is wrong or I own it and want it removed.

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, credited on the spot page. Email us the spot and we replace it; takedown requests for your own work are handled the same day.

Report a problem

Use the Feedback tab on the right edge of the app, or write to us. Please include your phone model, the app version (shown under Account → About) and what you tapped before it went wrong. Screenshots help by email.
